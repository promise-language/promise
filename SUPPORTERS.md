## About this page

This page is updated by hand, usually within a week of a new sponsorship. If you
have sponsored and don't see your name, open an issue or email
`hello@promise-lang.org` — it's an oversight, not a judgment.

Sponsors are listed by their GitHub account name unless they ask otherwise, and
anyone who prefers to sponsor privately is welcome to; a private sponsorship is
worth exactly as much to the project as a public one.

**Listing here is a thank-you, not an endorsement, in either direction.**
Sponsorship buys no influence over the language's design, the roadmap, what gets
merged, or the project's governance. Those are decided on technical merit
through the process in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md), and they stay that way
regardless of who is funding the work.

Promise is developed by Promise Lang LLC, a Texas company that holds the
project's intellectual property and keeps it permissively licensed under both
the Apache License, Version 2.0 and the MIT License. Sponsorships are ordinary
business income to the Company, **not** tax-deductible charitable donations.
